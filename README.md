# SafetyFilePro by Firebase Studio

SafetyFilePro is a comprehensive, secure, and intelligent platform designed to streamline the management of safety documents and compliance for any project. It empowers teams to organize, track, and collaborate on critical safety files with ease and efficiency.

Key features include:

*   **Centralized Project Dashboards**: Manage all your projects from a single, intuitive interface, with clear visibility into document status and approval progress.
*   **Intelligent Document Management**: Upload, categorize, and track all safety documents. Our AI-powered tagging automatically suggests relevant keywords, making retrieval effortless.
*   **Streamlined Workflows with Templates**: Kickstart new projects and documents in seconds using a library of pre-built, sector-specific templates.
*   **Secure External Collaboration**: Share projects with external stakeholders for approval and signing using secure, single-use links, ensuring a controlled and auditable review process.
*   **Compliance Calendar**: Never miss a deadline. An integrated calendar provides a clear overview of all document expiry dates, helping you stay ahead of compliance requirements.

SafetyFilePro is built to reduce administrative overhead, enhance collaboration, and ensure your safety compliance is always up-to-date.
